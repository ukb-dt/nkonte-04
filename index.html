Term Scientific/Math Mapping Lived/Gospel Mapping The "Ukhona" Meaning

$E(t \mid x) + \epsilon$ Expected Value “Hope / Vision” The system’s forecast of its own persistence.
$\frac{dE_x}{dt}$ Flux / Throughput “Toil / Snare” The actual experience of time passing and energy moving.
$\frac{d^2E_x}{dt^2}$ Curvature / Sensitivity “The Mind / Heart” Where regulation happens. If this term blows up, the system "loses its mind."

$\int E_x dt$ Accumulation “Testimony / Wisdom” The total sum of what was survived.

$\epsilon_x t$ Stochastic Drift “Scars / Aging” The memory of the noise; the reason we aren't the same after the storm.
